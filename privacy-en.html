GestorOS Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 10, 2026

This Privacy Policy describes how the GestorOS application ("the App") handles information. By installing and using the App you agree to the practices described here.

1. Controller

GestorOS is a service-order management app for repair shops. The developer can be contacted at YOUR-EMAIL@EXAMPLE.COM.

2. Data the App handles

The App runs locally on the user's device. Information entered manually by the user may include: customer name, phone, email and address; service-order details (equipment, reported issue, amounts, dates, status); and internal user name, login and hashed password.

3. Storage

All data is stored exclusively on the device in an encrypted SQLite database (SQLCipher) located in the app data folder. The App does not send this data to any developer server.

4. Third parties

The developer does not collect, access or share your data. The App contains no advertising, tracking or telemetry. WhatsApp sharing happens only when the user triggers it, through the installed WhatsApp app or WhatsApp Web, subject to WhatsApp/Meta policies. Generated PDFs are saved locally in the user's Documents folder.

5. Purpose (GDPR/LGPD)

Data is processed solely to let the user issue and manage service orders. The user is the controller of the customer data they insert into the App.

6. Retention and deletion

Data remains on the device until the user deletes it, for example by using in-app deletion functions or removing the database.

7. Security

The database can be password-protected (SQLCipher encryption) and internal user passwords are stored only as cryptographic hashes.

8. Children

The App is intended for professional use and is not directed at anyone under 18 years old.

9. Your rights

Under the GDPR and Brazil's LGPD (Law 13.709/2018), data subjects may request access, correction, portability and deletion. Because the data is under the direct control of the user/shop, such requests should be directed to them.

10. Changes

This policy may be updated; the last-updated date will always be shown at the top.

11. Contact

Privacy questions: YOUR-EMAIL@EXAMPLE.COM.
